CarbonNxt is a specialist carbon credit advisory and exchange desk. We manage the end-to-end lifecycle of verified carbon credits — including certification, sourcing, brokerage, trading, and transaction tracking.

We align with leading globally recognized registries and standards, including Verra (VCS), Gold Standard, American Carbon Registry (ACR), Climate Action Reserve (CAR), Puro.earth, and other prominent standards, ensuring your carbon credits are verified and audit-proof.

Our specialist advisory team guides you from methodology selection and documentation design to validation and registration. We help compile audit-ready project records so your credits are verified and ready for market demand.

We operate with transparent pricing and real-time market intelligence, providing full transaction traceability. We source and execute transactions directly on your behalf without hidden spreads or raw, un-vetted pricing.

We handle both sides of the market. Our exchange desk helps you source and buy verified credits for ESG commitments or list, broker, and sell credits you currently hold to certified buyers.
