INTEGRITY BY DESIGN

Supporting Credible and Traceable Environmental Assets

CarbonNxt is designed to integrate with recognized carbon standards and registries, helping participants access relevant project, credit and transaction information within a structured digital environment.

Compliance support, verification records and audit trails are intended to make it easier for organizations to maintain clear documentation and participate with greater confidence.

Compliance Pillars

  • Standard Information: Display applicable carbon standard and methodology.
  • Registry References: Provide registry-linked project and credit information.
  • Verification Records: Organize validation and verification documentation.
  • Compliance Documentation: Maintain transaction records for internal audit.
  • Ownership Visibility: Track credit ownership and retirement status.
  • Audit-Ready Records: Preserve structured history for all key marketplace transactions.
