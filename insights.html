Carbon Market Basics

Start with the Essentials

What Is a Carbon Credit?

A carbon credit generally represents one tonne of carbon dioxide equivalent reduced, avoided or removed under an applicable project and accounting framework.

Why Project Quality Matters

Credits can differ by methodology, project type, geography, monitoring approach, permanence, co-benefits and other quality factors.

What Does Retirement Mean?

Retirement is the step used to take a credit out of circulation so it can no longer be traded again, where applicable to the transaction and claim.

Why Documentation Matters

Project references, verification records, transaction records and retirement evidence can be important for internal ESG reporting and external review.

Standards & Registries

Why Standards and Methodologies Matter

Carbon projects are assessed against defined methodologies and program requirements. Buyers and project developers should understand which framework applies, what evidence is required and how project status is documented.

01

Methodology Fit

The selected methodology should match the project activity and the type of climate outcome being claimed.

02

Monitoring & Verification

Project data, evidence and verification processes help demonstrate whether the project continues to meet applicable requirements.

03

Registry References

Registry-linked records can help provide project and credit traceability where available.

04

Use Care with Claims

Formal partnerships, accreditations or registry relationships should only be stated when they are actually confirmed.

Project Knowledge

Explore Carbon Projects by Climate Outcome

Different project types can contribute to carbon removal, emissions avoidance or emissions reduction. Understanding the pathway helps buyers compare opportunities and helps developers position projects more clearly.

Carbon Removal

Examples can include biochar, biological sequestration and other activities designed to remove carbon from the atmosphere.

Emissions Avoidance

Projects can prevent emissions that might otherwise occur under an established baseline scenario.

Emissions Reduction

Projects can reduce greenhouse-gas emissions from energy, transport, industrial or other operating systems.
