Project Impact Categories

Supporting Different Climate Outcomes

Projects can be organized according to the climate outcome they deliver, helping buyers and project developers understand where each methodology fits within the wider carbon market.

Carbon Removal

Projects designed to remove carbon dioxide from the atmosphere and store it for a meaningful period.

Emissions Avoidance

Projects that help prevent emissions that would otherwise have occurred under a baseline scenario.

Emissions Reduction

Projects that reduce the quantity of greenhouse-gas emissions generated by existing activities or systems.
