Yes. The seller journey includes support for project readiness, certification guidance and documentation before market access.

Can I enquire even if I do not know which carbon credits I need?

Yes. Buyers can start by sharing their goals, budget, preferred category or geography, and CarbonNxt can help narrow the options.

Yes. Brokers and intermediaries are included in the client-defined audience and can enquire about market access and transaction support.
